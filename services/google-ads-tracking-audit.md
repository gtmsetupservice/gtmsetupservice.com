## Why this is the usual place to start

Most people arrive with one symptom and assume it's one problem. In a local service
account it's rarely one problem. Call tracking and form tracking break in different
ways, Local Services Ads reports on a different basis than Search, and the thing you
noticed is often the third-most-expensive failure rather than the first.

The audit is $100 more than the [Rescue]({{ '/services/tracking-rescue/' | relative_url }})
and covers the whole account rather than one thread. If it turns up a single failure
and nothing else, we say so plainly.

## The reported-versus-booked comparison

This is the part most audits skip. We take your own record of jobs booked last month
— a spreadsheet, a whiteboard photo, whatever you actually keep — and set it against
what Google Ads reported. The gap between those two numbers is the thing your budget
decisions have been resting on.

You'll usually find the gap is not evenly distributed. One campaign is under-reporting
badly and the others are close. That's the campaign you've probably been starving.

## What the audit is not

It isn't campaign management, keyword research, or a bid strategy review. We don't
tell you what to spend. We make sure that when you decide what to spend, the numbers
in front of you are real.
