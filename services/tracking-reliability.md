## Why this is the flagship

The one-time services fix what is broken today. This one is about the fact that it
will break again — not because anyone is careless, but because tracking is fragile
by nature and nothing in Google Ads tells you when it stops.

The failure mode is always the same shape. Something changes on the site. A tag stops
firing. Google keeps spending, now optimising toward the campaigns that still report
conversions rather than the ones that produce work. Nobody notices for six weeks.
By the time someone does, the budget has already been moved in the wrong direction.

## What the monthly report actually shows

Not a dashboard. One page: what you spent, by campaign, against how many jobs you
booked. If the two don't line up, that's the first thing we investigate that month.

## Adding it to an existing retainer

Most clients don't buy this as a separate vendor relationship. It goes on as a line
item alongside whatever marketing work is already happening. If you'd rather it be
invoiced that way, say so and it will be.

## Pricing range

$297/month covers a single-market account with a normal set of conversion actions.
It moves toward $497 when there are multiple service areas, several landing pages in
rotation, or Local Services Ads running alongside Search. You'll be quoted a specific
number before anything starts — the range is not an invitation to find out later.
