## When this is the right thing to buy

Buy the Rescue when you have a specific, visible symptom. Conversions went to zero
last month. The call extension stopped counting. A form that used to email you
doesn't anymore. There's one thread to pull, and pulling it is the whole job.

If you're not sure whether anything is broken — you just have a nagging sense that
the numbers don't match the work — the [Tracking Audit]({{ '/services/google-ads-tracking-audit/' | relative_url }})
is the better starting point. It checks everything rather than one reported symptom,
and it costs $100 more.

## What happens after

Most failures have a cause that will recur: a site edit, a plugin update, a new
landing page that nobody added the tag to. The fix holds until the next one of those
happens. If you'd rather find out within a day than at the end of a quarter, the
[Tracking Reliability Retainer]({{ '/services/tracking-reliability/' | relative_url }})
watches for it. There's no obligation to take it, and we won't chase you about it.
